PERSONAL GROWTH

Personal Development

Lessons I have learned about money, discipline, growth and planning for my future.

01

The Richest Man in Babylon

George S. Clason's The Richest Man in Babylon Showcases lessons about saving, managing money, investing and developing financial discipline.

For this reflection, I focused on the chapters "Seven Cures for a Lean Purse" and "The Five Laws of Gold."

02

Seven Cures for a Lean Purse

My understanding and reflection on the seven lessons presented in this chapter.

01

Start Thy Purse to Fattening

This cure enlightes me on how to save better. Instead of spending everything i have, i can be considerate to save for another day. Even it is a small amount, saving will help my financial decision and better my spending in my future.

02

Control Thy Expenditures

Earning money is not enough if I spend it without thinking. I should understand the difference between my needs and my wants. It is easy to spend money on things I want, but controlling my expenses would allow me to save more and use my money for things that are actually important.

03

Make Thy Gold Multiply

This explains that money should be used in a way that can help accumilate more money over time.I should learn about saving and responsible investing. This reminds me that I should educate myself before making financial decisions so that I do not take unnecessary risks.

04

Guard Thy Treasures

I should protect the money i worked hard for. I will not make poor financial decisions that will affect me in the long run. In the future i want to be careful with whom i trust with my funds or an ivenstment.

05

Make Thy Dwelling Profitable

This made me think about the importance of having a substantial place to live. A home should be apart of good financial planning. In the future, I would like to make housing decisions that fit within my income and contribute positively to my financial stability.

06

Insure a Future Income

I want to prepare myself financially so that I can be secure later in life. Saving, building useful skills and planning for future needs can help me become more independent and prepared for unexpected situations.

07

Increase Thy Ability to Earn

This cure stands out to me because i believe my education and my skills can affect my future. I should continue to develop my skills, knowledge and oppurtunites. It is not only about attaining a degree but developing skills that can increase my potential.

03

The Five Laws of Gold

The lessons from this chapter that stood out to me.

1. Save at Least One-Tenth of Your Earnings

This teaches me that I should save a portion of the money I earn instead of spending everything. Saving consistently can help me prepare for emergencies and future goals.

2. Make Your Gold Work and Multiply

This means I should find responsible ways for my savings to grow instead of allowing my money to simply sit unused. I should learn about financial opportunities before making decisions.

3. Protect Your Gold Through Wise Investments

I should protect the money I have worked hard to earn. This means being careful with investments and avoiding financial decisions that I do not fully understand.

4. Do Not Invest in What You Do Not Understand

This cure reminds me of making poor decision. I should put my money to good use and in a proper investment. An investment im not sure of should not be where i put my funds.

5. Avoid Impossible or Unrealistic Earnings

This reminds me that improving my education, knowledge and skills can help me qualify for better opportunities. The more valuable skills I develop, the more opportunities I can create for myself.

04

How I Can Apply These Lessons

I can apply these lessons to my life by developing better financial habits and becoming more responsible with my money. Start by saving some of the money I receive instead of spending everything, while also learning to separate my needs from my wants. I can be more careful with financial decisions by researching before investing or trusting someone with my money. For my future i will settle financial goals and build savings for unexpected situations. Most importantly, I can continue improving my education and skills because increasing my knowledge and abilities that can help me qualify for better opportunities and increase my ability to earn in the future.

I want to become more intentional about the financial choices I make. One change I would like to make is keeping track of my spending so I can see where my money is actually going instead of wondering where it went. I also want to become less impulsive when buying things and give myself time to decide whether something is really worth the cost. Thirdly, learning more about financial planning so I can make smarter choices as I become more independent. These changes would help me develop self-discipline, reduce financial stress and give me more control over my future

MY BIGGEST TAKEAWAY

My biggest lesson taken from this book is you can make a change now. You do not have to wait unitl you earn a large some of money to start builiding a financial future. Being disciplined with spending, saving consistently and making informed decisions can make a difference over time. I should be more intentional with my money and think about how my decisions today affect my future.

I found this meaningful because the habits I develop now can influence my future. I plan to apply this lesson by becoming more mindful of my spending, and setting aside money for future goals and thinking carefully before making financial decisions.
