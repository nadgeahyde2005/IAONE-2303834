MY VIEW ON ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

LLMs Should Be a Tool, Not a Crutch

Large Language Models for example ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini have become useful tools for students. They help explain difficult concepts, provide examples, brainstorm ideas and assist students where necessary. I believe students should use these tools as support rather than allowing them to do all of the thinking.

As a university student, I use LLM to explain a topic that I do not understand, give me examples, help me identify mistakes in my work and suggest ways that I can improve my understanding. For example, when learning coding, I can explain to an AI tool to elaborate a difficult concept in simpler terms and then use my notes and other academic sources to verify what I learned.

LLMs can also be useful when learning programming. An AI tool can be used to complete an entire assignment but intstead its better to ask and explain an error in a code or expound what particular piece of code works. This allows me to learn from the problem instead of simply copying an answer.

However, relying too heavily on AI can negatively affect my education. If I allow an AI tool to complete all of my assignments, I may not achieve the adequate skills and knowledge to receive an answer without actually developing the knowledge and skills that I need.

To improve in my eductaion, I believe the best approach is to use AI when it helps me learn while still completing the thinking and learning myself. AI should help me become a better student rather than replace me as a student.
